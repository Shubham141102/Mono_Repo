Inventory Policy

1. Products with stock quantity at or below the reorder level should be reviewed for replenishment.

2. Products with zero stock require immediate replenishment attention.

3. High-demand products should receive higher replenishment priority than low-demand products.

4. Products with consistently increasing demand should be monitored closely to avoid stockouts.

5. Inventory decisions should consider both current stock quantity and recent demand patterns.

6. A product should not be treated as high priority solely because of low stock if its recent demand is consistently low.

7. Store-level inventory should be evaluated independently because product demand can vary significantly between stores.
