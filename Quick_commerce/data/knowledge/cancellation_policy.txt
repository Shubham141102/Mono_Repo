Cancellation Policy

1. Cancelled orders should be monitored to identify recurring operational or customer-related issues.

2. Cancellation rate should be evaluated relative to total orders.

3. A high number of cancellations at a particular store should trigger further investigation.

4. Cancellation patterns should be evaluated by time period to identify recurring trends.

5. Cancellation analysis should consider payment method, delivery type, store, and customer segment where the data is available.

6. A single cancelled order should not be treated as evidence of a systemic problem.

7. Repeated cancellation patterns should be investigated before operational changes are recommended.
