Delivery Policy

1. Delivery performance should be evaluated using delivery duration, delivery status, and delivery completion rate.

2. Delivered orders should be used when calculating completed delivery performance.

3. Orders that are cancelled should not be treated as successfully delivered orders.

4. Delivery partners with consistently longer delivery times should be reviewed for operational issues.

5. Store-level delivery performance should be monitored because delivery performance can vary between locations.

6. High delivery volume should be considered when evaluating delivery performance.

7. Delivery metrics should be interpreted together with order volume rather than using delivery time alone.
