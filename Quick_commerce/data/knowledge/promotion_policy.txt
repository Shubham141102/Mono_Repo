Promotion Policy

1. Promotions should be evaluated based on their effect on sales volume and revenue.

2. Discount levels should be monitored because larger discounts do not necessarily result in proportionally higher revenue.

3. Promotional performance should be compared across products and stores.

4. Products with strong demand and effective promotional response can be considered for continued promotional activity.

5. Low-demand products should not automatically receive larger discounts without evaluating their historical performance.

6. Promotional decisions should consider product price, discount percentage, sales quantity, and revenue together.

7. Promotion performance should be evaluated over comparable time periods.
